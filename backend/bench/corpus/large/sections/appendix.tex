\section{Derivation of the mixing weight update}\label{sec:derive-pi}

Maximising the expected complete-data log-likelihood under the constraint
$\sum_j \pi_j = 1$ uses a Lagrange multiplier $\lambda$. Setting the derivative
of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lagrange}
  \mathcal{L} = \sum_{i,j} r_{ij}\log \pi_j - \lambda\Bigl(\sum_j \pi_j - 1\Bigr)
\end{equation}
with respect to $\pi_j$ to zero gives $\pi_j = \sum_i r_{ij}/\lambda$. Summing
over $j$ shows $\lambda = n$, which recovers Equation~\eqref{eq:upd-pi}.
