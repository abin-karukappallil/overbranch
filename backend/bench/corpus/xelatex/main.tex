\begin{abstract}
  This document is a workload for the XeLaTeX path of the OverBranch compiler
  benchmark. It uses \texttt{fontspec} for system-level font selection,
  \texttt{unicode-math} for OpenType mathematics, and direct UTF-8 input for
  Greek letters, accented Latin text and mathematical symbols.
\end{abstract}

\section{Unicode text}

Plain text may contain accented Latin letters such as café, naïve, Zürich,
Ångström and Łódź, as well as Greek words like λόγος and
φύσις. Typographic punctuation such as “curly quotes”, em
dashes —, and the ellipsis … are all handled directly by the engine.

The Greek alphabet is used heavily in mathematics: $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$,
$\Gamma$, $\Delta$, $\Omega$, $\varepsilon$, $\lambda$, $\pi$ and $\sigma$
all appear as glyphs from the math font.

\section{Mathematical symbols}

Symbols from several blocks can be typed directly into the source:
$\forall x \in \mathbb{R}\;\exists\, y \ge x$, $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J}$,
$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^{-2} = \tfrac{\pi^2}{6}$ and $\hbar \approx 1.0546 \times 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$.

Set operations and logic: $A \cup B \subseteq C$, $A \cap \varnothing = \varnothing$,
$p \Rightarrow q \Leftrightarrow \lnot q \Rightarrow \lnot p$.

\section{Display equations}

The Gaussian integral is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gauss}
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}.
\end{equation}

Euler's identity follows from the power series of the exponential:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:euler}
  e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta,
  \qquad\text{so}\qquad
  e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0.
\end{equation}

A matrix-valued expression with Greek entries:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:matrix}
  \Sigma =
  \begin{pmatrix}
    \sigma_{11} & \rho\,\sigma_1\sigma_2 \\
    \rho\,\sigma_1\sigma_2 & \sigma_{22}
  \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad -1 \le \rho \le 1.
\end{equation}

Equation~\eqref{eq:gauss} is the normalisation constant of the Gaussian density,
and Equation~\eqref{eq:euler} relates it to complex exponentials.
Equation~\eqref{eq:matrix} is positive semidefinite whenever $|\rho| \le 1$.

\section{Mixed fonts}

Sans-serif text can mark a heading inside a paragraph, \textsf{like this},
and monospace is available for code: \texttt{x = \textbackslash alpha}.
Colour is available through \textcolor{teal}{xcolor}.
